Categories & rules
Each category carries a product kind plus optional defaults: a shelf life for expiration estimates and a storage location for new units. When a unit has no printed date, its expiration is estimated from the nearest rule — a subcategory rule wins; otherwise it inherits the parent’s. “No expiration default” is a valid state.
